Privacy Policy

ReadFirst · last updated: 19 November 2026 · Română

In short

  • ReadFirst lets parents give children screen time in exchange for reading.
  • No ads, no analytics or tracking tools, and we never sell or rent data.
  • Children don't create accounts or enter personal information.
  • Unless you turn on the optional “Account & family” feature, all data stays on the phone.
  • Parents can delete all data at any time from inside the app.

We don't collect location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data beyond the features below. Optional reading aloud asks for microphone permission when the child first taps “Start reading”. The audio is processed by the phone's speech-recognition service (usually Google); the app only receives the recognized text, compares it with the sentence on the page and keeps neither audio nor text. QR scanning uses the Google Play services scanner; the app gets no camera access.

Scanning a paper book (optional, parent mode only): the pages are photographed with the Google Play services document scanner or chosen from the gallery with the system photo picker, so the app has no camera permission and sees only the pages you scan or pick. The text is recognized on the phone itself, by Google's ML Kit library built into the app. The photos are not sent to us or to anyone else, The recognized text becomes a book in the library. A smaller copy of each page photo is kept next to the book, in the app's own storage on this phone only, so the original page (layout, pictures, exponents) can be shown while reading; it is never sent to us or anyone else and is deleted with the book. The scanner's temporary files are deleted right away (photos you picked stay in your own gallery).

Who we share data with

Nobody, except our infrastructure provider Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore), which processes data on our behalf. Book searches are sent to Project Gutenberg (gutenberg.org, with Gutendex as a backup) and to Romanian Wikisource (ro.wikisource.org, run by the Wikimedia Foundation). Books are downloaded from gutenberg.org or, when it is unavailable, from the Project Gutenberg servers gutenberg.pglaf.org and aleph.pglaf.org (book covers also come from gutenberg.pglaf.org); Wikisource books are downloaded from ro.wikisource.org or, as a backup, from the Wikisource export tool (ws-export.wmcloud.org). These requests contain only the search text or the title of the book, never data about the child; like any website, these services see the phone's IP address. Google's ML Kit library, used for scanning paper books, may send Google technical usage statistics (such as performance and device model), but never the photos or the recognized text.

Retention and deletion

  • Data on the phone is kept until you uninstall the app or use Parent mode → Privacy & data → Delete data on this phone.
  • The account and family data in the cloud are kept until you use Delete account and cloud data. Deletion is immediate and permanent, and covers all linked children.
  • You can also request deletion by email at the address above.

Children

The app is installed and set up by a parent or guardian, who gives consent the first time the app is opened. Children don't create accounts, see no ads, can't buy anything and can't communicate with other people through the app. Children's data is used only so the parent can follow their reading and set the rules.

Your rights (GDPR)

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority. Processing is based on the parent's consent and on providing the service the parent asked for.

Special permissions on the child's phone

  • Usage access: so the app knows which app is open and can apply the rules.
  • Display over other apps: for the “Reading break” screen over a blocked app.
  • Device admin (optional): so the child can't easily uninstall the app.
  • Microphone (optional): only for reading aloud, only while the child is reading a sentence.
  • Foreground service: so protection keeps running in the background, with a persistent notification.
